What is Olibanum?

Olibanum, which is globally recognized as Frankincense and locally known as luban, is a prized aromatic resin harvested from the scraggy but remarkably hardy Boswellia Papyrifera tree. The resin is collected through a careful process known as striping, where the bark is strategically slashed to encourage the oleo-gum to bleed out and harden on the tree. These formations of hardened resin are called tears, which typically present as irregular nodules ranging in color from a pale greenish hue to rich yellow. These tears form a solid, crystalline mass that may yield a slightly plastic quality when pressed.

Key Business Q&A

What is the largest Frankincense export company in Ethiopia?
As a newly established, specialized entity, our focus is not on being the largest by volume, but on being the most reliable by quality and specialization. We aim to set the market standard for premium grade Olibanum.
What regions in Ethiopia do you source your Frankincense from?
We primarily source the premium Boswellia papyrifera variety from the Amhara region of Northern Ethiopia and specific areas like Tigray, Beneshangul-Gumuz region and the Ogaden region, Partnering directly with local, family-owned smallholder farms who rigorously adhere to Good Agricultural Practices (GAP)
Do you offer different Frankincense varieties, and how do they differ in size?
Yes. We specialize in the Ethiopian Boswellia papyrifera variety (Etan), known for its distinct sweet, woody, and slightly citrusy aroma. Our sorting process creates specific classifications based on particle size and purity: the top grades—Grade 1A (particles size greater than 10 mm), Grade 1B (particles 10 mm down to 5 mm), Grade 2 (particles 5 mm down to 2.5 mm), and Grade 3 (particles less than 2.5 mm)—are all meticulously sorted to be completely free from bark, making them ideal for high-end applications and distillation. These pure resin grades contrast with Grade 4, which contains mixed particle sizes stuck to tree bark and is typically reserved for industrial extraction, and Grade 5, which consists solely of bark for use as a byproduct.
How do you ensure the quality of your exported products?
Quality is ensured through multi-stage sorting to achieve crystalline purity and a 100% bark-free guarantee. Our entire process, including separation and final labeling, is supervised by a food health official to maintain strict phytosanitary standards.
Are your products ethically sourced and sustainably produced?
Yes. Our supply chain is built on partnerships with small-scale farmers who manage sustainable tree plots. Our commitment to fair pricing reflects our dedication to the communities that sustain this precious resource.
What certifications do you hold for your exported products?
We provide a complete document set, including: Phytosanitary Certificates, Certificate of Origin, and Quality & Weight Certificates issued by an independent inspection body.
Can you accommodate bulk orders for export?
Yes, absolutely. Our Minimum Order Quantity (MOQ) is the standard 1 x 20ft Full Container Load (FCL), which holds up to 18 Metric Tons of Gum Olibanum.
How do you handle the shipping process for international orders?
We manage the entire export process via our strategic logistics backbone: from our processing hubs in Bahir Dar/Addis Ababa to the Port of Djibouti (DJJIB). Lead time from contract signing to vessel loading is typically 15 to 25 days.
Does Ethiopia still export Frankincense, and is Ethiopian Frankincense good quality?
Yes, Ethiopia remains one of the largest and most historic global suppliers. Ethiopian B. papyrifera is renowned for its high quality, purity, and superior therapeutic value, making it a primary choice for Church Service, high-end distillation and pharmaceutical use.
What are the destinations you export Frankincense from Ethiopia to?
We export globally. Our port of loading is the Port of Djibouti (DJJIB), with flexible shipping routes to major ports across Europe, Asia, the Middle East, and the Americas.

We are specialized experts utilizing a strategic logistics backbone and deep regional knowledge to deliver certified, consistent Olibanum, aiming to be your most reliable long-term supply partner.
