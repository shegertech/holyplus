Grade 1B

Product Details

Premium Classification

Our Grade 1B represents certified quality derived entirely from the Boswellia papyrifera tree.

Grade 1B consists of medium-sized resin tears selected for their uniform diameter and exceptional aromatic strength. Harvested by hand-slashing the Boswellia papyrifera bark and collected once dry, this grade is subjected to intensive sorting to verify zero bark inclusions and low dust content.

Primary Applications: Widely used in essential oil steam distillation, wellness extract manufacturing, and mid-tier cosmetic blending.
