Meticulous Sorting

Bazen Grades

We classify our Boswellia papyrifera (Etan) resin into six specific premium grades based on particle size and purity. Each grade is meticulously sorted to be completely free from bark and organic impurities.

Size: > 10 mm

Grade 1A

Sourced exclusively from the renowned Boswellia papyrifera tree in the highlands. Large, beautiful tears. 100% bark-free. Ideal for luxury perfumery, spiritual incense, and aromatherapy.

Broad Industry Applications

General Uses of Gum Olibanum

Frankincense is a versatile organic resin with uses spanning wellness, beauty, and ritual applications.

  • Aromatherapy & Perfumery Highly aromatic essential oils are obtained from raw resins by steam distillation, serving as premium fragrances and mood-enhancing anxiety reducers.
  • Nutraceutical & Traditional Medicine Edible Bazen resin has anti-inflammatory properties, serving traditional medicine applications for joint health, cell support, and digestion.
  • Cosmetics & Skincare Abundant in chemical compounds like boswellic acids, the resin serves as a critical ingredient in restorative skincare creams and anti-aging soap making.
  • Sting Relief & Topicals Used locally to provide therapeutic relief from insect and scorpion stings due to natural soothing attributes.
