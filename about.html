Our Background

Company Profile & Heritage

The founding vision of Hoy Plus Trading took root in 2024, built upon a rich, collective knowledge of Ethiopia's natural resource sector.

Through our dedicated brand, Bazen Ethiopian Frankincense, our sole mission is to export Ethiopia’s unique, high-quality natural gums, specializing in premium Frankincense (Gum Olibanum) to clients around the world.

By centering our logistics backbone within the core producing territories of Ethiopia, we ensure a reliable, steady supply chain for our global buyers. Our success is underpinned by our staff—a team of professionals who possess deep knowledge and practical experience in resin sourcing, agricultural logistics, and international business management.

Core Pillars

✦ Specialization: Focused solely on Gum Olibanum and Ethiopian resins, not generalized spices.

✦ Integrity: Hand-sorted crystalline purity with verified laboratory checks.

✦ Partnership: Long-term supply security rather than quick spot-market transactions.

The Strategic Backbone

Our central hub and headquarter offices in Addis Ababa and operational hubs in Bahir Dar position us close to primary harvesting clusters. This network ensures maximum logistics efficiency.

Professional Expertise

Our team possesses deep knowledge in agricultural logistics, resin harvesting, and international trade compliance. We constantly train staff to maintain strict quality standards.

Building Partnerships

We believe trading is built on long-term relationships. We maintain active communications, local/international trade presence, and a responsive feedback loop to support partners worldwide.

Ethical Commitments

Our business practices respect both the environment and the livelihoods of forest communities:

  • Fair Farm Gate Pricing: We protect local incomes, promoting economic resilience in harvesting areas.
  • Managed Tree Plots: Supporting striping rotations to allow Boswellia stands to regenerate naturally.
  • Community-First Sourcing: Sourcing entirely from smallholders, preventing commercial over-exploitation.
Social & Ecological Responsibility

Sustainability & Ethical Sourcing

Gum Olibanum is a natural forest resource harvested from fragile wild habitats. At Bazen, we understand that protecting the future of the Boswellia papyrifera species is essential for both the ecosystem and our long-term supply agreements.

Our supply chain is built on cooperative partnerships with smallholder family farms who manage sustainable wild tree plots. By providing fair, direct market pricing, we encourage farmers to protect these hardy trees from over-tapping and clear-cutting.

Every kilogram of Bazen Frankincense is sourced with care, respecting the delicate highlands and the traditional tapping knowledge of local communities.

Ancient Abyssinia Legacy

About Ethiopia: Sourcing Origin

Ethiopia is the definitive historical home of premium aromatic resins. Anchored in a 4,000-year legacy of global trade dating back to ancient Abyssinia, the country remains a premier African exporter of incense and gums.

The dry, mountainous terrains of the Ethiopian Highlands provide the perfect, arid habitat for the unique Boswellia papyrifera trees. The resin harvested here, known locally as Etan, possesses inherent characteristics:

  • Sweet & Citrusy: An aromatic profile containing distinct bright, woody, and lemon-like citrus notes.
  • Chemical Richness: Abundant in therapeutic terpenes and highly effective boswellic acids, making it highly sought after for global wellness brands.
  • High-Grade Yield: Dry-altitude conditions encourage the tree to bleed out a crystalline resin that hardens into pale yellow tears.

Ethiopian Etan at a Glance

"Unlike heavier resin types, B. papyrifera (Etan) from the Ethiopian Highlands stands out for its high terpene value, bright citrus notes, and therapeutic purity."

Operational Advantage

Warehouse Visit & Facility Operations

Our primary competitive advantage is built on domestic infrastructure. We run strategically located warehouse operations in both Bahir Dar and Addis Ababa, establishing a secure chain of custody from the tapping tree to the ocean freight container.

When raw, wild-harvested resin tears arrive at our Bahir Dar warehouse, they undergo a primary manual sorting. Dust and large organic debris are separated immediately.

The sorted resin is then moved under climate-controlled logistics to our Addis Ababa headquarters. Here, secondary grading is performed using mechanical sieves and a dedicated manual inspection crew. The tears are separated into Grades 1A, 1B, 2, and 3, sealed in 50 kg PP bags, and stored in a temperature-controlled environment, ensuring they do not merge or lose volatile oil quality prior to customs container load-out.

Sourcing & Quality Q&A

Which regions in Ethiopia do you source your Frankincense from?

+

We primarily source the premium Boswellia papyrifera variety from the Amhara region of Northern Ethiopia and specific areas like Tigray, Beneshangul-Gumuz, and the Ogaden region. We partner directly with local smallholder farms who rigorously adhere to Good Agricultural Practices (GAP).

Do you offer different Frankincense size classifications?

+

Yes. Our sorting process creates specific classifications based on particle size and purity: Grade 1A (>10 mm tears), Grade 1B (5–10 mm), Grade 2 (2.5–5 mm), and Grade 3 (<2.5 mm). These are meticulously sorted to be 100% bark-free. Grade 4 contains mixed particle sizes stuck to tree bark (for industrial extraction), and Grade 5 consists solely of bark.

How do you ensure the quality of your exported products?

+

Quality is ensured through multi-stage manual and sieve sorting to achieve crystalline purity and a 100% bark-free guarantee. Our entire process, including separation, grading, and final labeling, is supervised by a certified food health official to maintain phytosanitary standards.
